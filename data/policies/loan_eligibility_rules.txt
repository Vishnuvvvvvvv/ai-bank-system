Loan Eligibility Rules

The AI assistant may explain loan rules from policy, but final eligibility must be calculated by deterministic banking services.

Home loan rules:
- Minimum monthly salary: INR 30,000.
- Minimum average balance: INR 50,000.
- KYC is mandatory.
- Salary slip and bank statement are required before application creation.

Bike loan rules:
- Minimum monthly salary: INR 20,000.
- KYC is mandatory.
- Salary slip is required before application creation.

Personal loan rules:
- Minimum monthly salary: INR 25,000.
- KYC is mandatory.
- Healthy transaction history is required.
- Salary slip and bank statement are required before application creation.
