Debit Card Policy

Customers with an active savings account may request a debit card.

Card operations:
- A customer may block a lost or compromised ATM/debit card immediately.
- A temporarily frozen card can be reactivated after customer verification.
- Card activation requires an active linked account.

Security rules:
- Suspicious usage may trigger temporary freeze.
- The bank never asks for PIN, OTP, or full card CVV over chat.
