Bank Loan Products

The bank provides multiple loan products.

Available loans:
- Home Loan
- Personal Loan
- Car Loan
- Education Loan
- Gold Loan
- Business Loan

Home Loan:
- Interest rate starts from 8.5%
- Maximum tenure: 30 years

Personal Loan:
- Interest rate starts from 11%
- No collateral required

Car Loan:
- Financing available up to 90% of vehicle value

Education Loan:
- Available for domestic and international studies

Gold Loan:
- Loan issued against pledged gold

Business Loan:
- Loans for startups and enterprises
