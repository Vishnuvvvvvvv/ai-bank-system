HOME LOAN POLICY

Eligibility Criteria:
- Minimum monthly salary should be ₹30,000
- Minimum work experience should be 2 years
- Applicant age must be between 21 and 60
- Maximum EMI burden allowed is 50 percent
- Both salaried and self-employed applicants are eligible

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Salary Slips
- Bank Statements
- Address Proof

Interest Rate:
- Starting from 8.5 percent annually
