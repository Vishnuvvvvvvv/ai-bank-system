Savings Account Information

A savings account allows customers to safely
deposit money while earning interest.

Features:
- Secure money storage
- ATM/debit card access
- Online banking access
- Interest earnings
- Mobile banking support

Documents required to open a savings account:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport size photo
- Address proof
- Mobile number

Steps to open a savings account:
1. Submit KYC documents
2. Fill account opening form
3. Complete verification
4. Deposit minimum opening balance

Minimum balance requirement:
₹5000 for regular savings account.

Types of savings accounts:
- Regular Savings Account
- Salary Account
- Student Savings Account
- Senior Citizen Savings Account
