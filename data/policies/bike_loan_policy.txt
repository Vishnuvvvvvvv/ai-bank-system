Bike Loan Policy

Bike loans are offered for two-wheeler purchases by salaried customers with verified KYC.

Eligibility rules:
- Minimum monthly salary: INR 20,000.
- KYC is mandatory through Aadhaar or PAN.
- A recent salary slip is required for income validation.
- Loan amount and tenure are subject to bank underwriting rules.

Documents accepted:
- Salary slip.
- Aadhaar or PAN.
- Vehicle quotation or invoice when available.
